Why one QR, and why it never changes

The QR on every Astraea package points here — to its product's lab page — and it stays the same for every batch we ever make. It never encodes a specific lot, so it can never send you to the wrong certificate. You choose your batch from the list and see exactly what was in the pack in your hand.

Testing is performed by an independent, ISO 17025-accredited laboratory. Astraea does not own, operate, or pay the lab based on results. If a batch fails, it doesn't ship, and its lot number is retired.
