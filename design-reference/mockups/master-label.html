Design decisions

The facts panel is the hero of zone 2Set in regulation black-on-white Helvetica inside its rule box — deliberately untouched by brand styling. Surrounding it with warm paper and letting it sit stark signals: this part isn't marketing. The "Free from / no proprietary blends" block sits directly beneath it, where label-readers' eyes already are.
"Our Promise" replaces pufferyWhere competitors put adjectives, zone 3 carries one falsifiable sentence about this lot's testing — and points back to the QR. Every claim on the label is either a regulated fact or checkable via the scan.
The lunar line does double duty"28 capsules · 28-day supply · one lunar cycle" is simultaneously the FDA net-quantity statement territory, the subscription rhythm, and the only poetic flourish on the label. One line, three jobs.
Evergreen art, stamped variablesNothing batch-specific is printed in the label art — lot and best-by are inkjet-stamped in the seam zone at filling. One label SKU serves every production run; the QR resolves server-side to the current lot's page via the stamped code entry.

Compliance checklist (per label)

  • Statement of identity ("Herbal Dietary Supplement") on the principal display panel — 21 CFR 101.3
  • Net quantity of contents ("28 Capsules") on the front panel — 21 CFR 101.105
  • Supplement Facts panel in prescribed format: serving size, servings per container, amounts, %DV or † — 21 CFR 101.36
  • Other-ingredients list in descending order below the facts box
  • Structure/function claims accompanied by the FDA disclaimer, verbatim, in a bordered box
  • Distributor name and full place of business
  • Allergen declaration per FALCPA (or "free from" statement where truthful)
  • Lot identification on every unit (stamped) — 21 CFR 111 traceability
  • Final compliance pass by a regulatory consultant before print — required for all 26 SKUs
